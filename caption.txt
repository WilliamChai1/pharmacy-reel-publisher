Parents and grandparents, let’s talk about something we’ve *all* probably done in the kitchen—using normal dining spoons to measure out cough syrup or medicine for the little ones. 

Did you know that standard kitchen teaspoons and tablespoons vary wildly in size? Using them is a recipe for accidental under-dosing (meaning the medicine won't work) or, even worse, dangerous overdosing. 

As your friendly neighborhood pharmacist right here in Kuching, I want to keep our little ones safe and thriving! Here is some quick, practical advice for the family:

• **Ditch the cutlery:** Never use kitchen spoons for liquid medicine. They are not calibrated for medical use.
• **Use proper tools:** Always use the dosing syringe, measuring cup, or dropper that comes with the medication. 
• **Ask us for help:** If your medicine didn't come with a measure, drop by our counter—we’ll happily provide you with a proper oral syringe for free!
