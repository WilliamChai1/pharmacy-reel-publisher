Let’s be honest, *ah*—when our little ones fall sick, getting them to take their cough or fever syrup can feel like a mini battle. And in the rush, it's so easy to just grab whatever spoon is nearest in the kitchen drawer. 

Did you know that everyday dining spoons come in all shapes and sizes, and using them is actually one of the most common ways we accidentally under-dose or over-dose our children? *Aiyoh*, we definitely don't want that! 

Watch this quick, fun cartoon reel to see why kitchen spoons are a no-go, and how you can make medicine time a breeze. 🥄✨

Here is what you need to know, parents:
• **Ditch the dining spoon:** Kitchen spoons hold vastly different amounts of liquid. Always use the proper oral syringe or dosing cup that comes with the medicine.
• **Dose by weight, not age:** Children grow at different rates! Always calculate the correct syrup dosage based on your child's current weight for safety and effectiveness.
• **Ask your pharmacist:** Not sure how to read the markings on the syringe? Drop by and we will gladly show you the right way in seconds.
